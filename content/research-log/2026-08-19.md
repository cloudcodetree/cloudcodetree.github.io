nothing about what a design post may be ABOUT.

**Contract changes:**
- **The subject is a workflow, never a product.** A design post covers something
  the reader does — a handoff, a decision, a review, a token structure — and
  should still make sense with a different tool substituted. Claude Design and
  Claude Code are the default pair, being the round trip this reader runs. Other
  tools may appear as supporting detail inside a technique; a post whose subject
  is a third-party product is out.
- **No release announcements in the design bucket at all.** If a design tool ships
  something that changes this reader's workflow, it goes in the news bucket with a
  "why it matters" line; the design bucket can cover the technique once one exists.
- Tell (c) is now applied literally to the title: delete the product name and read
  it back. "___ Is Now Generally Available" collapses to nothing.

**The rules are working elsewhere, worth recording.** The same 08-19 run also
produced two tool-neutral posts hitting angles named in the contract — DTCG design
tokens an agent can consume, and regenerate-vs-edit-in-your-editor. Across every
design post to date the pattern is consistent: every removal was vendor-as-subject,
every keeper is technique-as-subject.

Design ramp-up 8/12 → **7/12**.
