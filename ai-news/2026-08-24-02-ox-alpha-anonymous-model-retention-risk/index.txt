OX Alpha accepts text, image, and video input with tool-calling support. On DeepSWE (an agentic coding benchmark), it scores 80%, ranking above GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 5 in that specific test. Community fingerprinting published August 22 points with high confidence to Zhipu AI's GLM infrastructure — likely GLM-5.3 — but no lab has officially claimed it.

**The retention problem**

The per-model data notice and the OpenRouter stealth-model listing give different answers. One access route (OpenCode) says zero retention; the OpenRouter listing says the anonymous provider retains prompts and completions. That contradiction was still unresolved as of August 23.

The practical guidance: OX Alpha is useful for benchmarking your own tasks against what is achievable at free pricing. Running it against your own open-source code or synthetic evals is low-risk and informative. Keep customer data, proprietary source code, and credentials off it until the operator is publicly identified and the retention story is consistent.

**The free window**

The provider stated the free pricing window as approximately one week from August 20. Watch [the OpenRouter listing](https://openrouter.ai/ox/alpha) for an identity disclosure or pricing announcement this week — that is when the cost-per-token story will become clear.

**Why it matters:** an 80% DeepSWE score from an anonymous model is worth tracking — if the operator surfaces and retention terms clarify, it becomes a serious option for coding tasks. For now: benchmark on it, do not ship with it.
