12:T75a,**Two items for engineers building agents: a benchmark that checks what your agent actually changed in the database, and a developer conference worth watching on September 29.**

**Microsoft ThinkingBox (released Aug 19, open source):** An eval sandbox for testing AI agents on business-process tasks — order processing, inventory updates, scheduling. The key distinction: instead of grading an agent on what it *says* it did, ThinkingBox checks what it actually changed in a connected database. Microsoft tested 12 models across 507 tasks spanning five business domains; pass rates varied widely among models that look equivalent on standard coding benchmarks. Available at [github.com/microsoft/thinkingbox](https://github.com/microsoft/thinkingbox).

**Why it matters:** Claim-vs-result divergence is the most common failure pattern in production business agents. If you're choosing a model for a workflow that writes to a real system, running candidates through ThinkingBox on representative tasks gives you actual completion rates rather than benchmark proxy scores.

**OpenAI DevDay 2026:** September 29 at Fort Mason, San Francisco. In-person applications are closed; the opening keynote will be livestreamed. First year the conference adds satellite DevDay Exchange events in eight cities (Bengaluru, Tokyo, Seoul, Paris, Berlin, London, São Paulo, Mexico City). Historically the venue for significant API and platform announcements.
