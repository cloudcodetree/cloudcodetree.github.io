12:T874,**TL;DR: New data shows Google DeepMind's share of top European AI research hires fell from 49% (2022–23) to 18.6% (2025–26). AlphaFold co-creator John Jumper joined Anthropic; Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer joined OpenAI; AlphaGo researcher David Silver founded Ineffable Intelligence.**

New analysis published today by Fortune quantifies what had been visible as individual departures: DeepMind's grip on European AI research talent has loosened significantly. The exits include Jeff Dean (27-year Google veteran), Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals and Quoc Le (who launched Discovery Loop), AlphaFold co-creator John Jumper (now at Anthropic, along with AlphaFold researchers Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel), and Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer (now at OpenAI). David Silver, the RL researcher behind AlphaGo, founded Ineffable Intelligence, now valued at $5.1 billion.

Researchers who left cite frustration with DeepMind's shift from open-ended science toward Google product delivery and stricter publication restrictions introduced in 2025.

**Why it matters:** Frontier model capability tends to follow research talent. Anthropic is capturing a notable share of DeepMind alumni — Jumper's AlphaFold work is foundational to modern protein-structure and multimodal reasoning research. For engineers deciding where to invest workflow time and which models to build around: the concentration of research expertise is shifting toward Anthropic and OpenAI, and that directionally affects where capability advances are most likely to land first.

**Limits to note:** talent movement doesn't directly predict model quality — many breakthroughs have come from small, focused teams, and Google retains world-class compute infrastructure. Hiring data lags the actual model roadmap by 12–24 months at minimum, so DeepMind's Gemini roadmap is not suddenly derailed.
